# Tier 14 — Governance Attack Scenarios

_Tabletop exercises for coordination crises. Pick one per month, walk through with the team._

---

## Scenario 1: Critical consensus bug in live mainnet
- Who gets the 3 AM email? Is security@ monitored?
- How do you verify without running exploit on mainnet?
- How do you coordinate fix without leaking?
- How do you reach exchanges/pools for mandatory upgrade?

## Scenario 2: Team lead's signing key compromised
- How do you confirm compromise vs. cloned infrastructure?
- How do you revoke? Who signs the revocation?
- What about users who already installed malicious version?

## Scenario 3: 51% attack detected in real time
- How did you detect? Does monitoring alert?
- Do you disclose publicly or investigate privately?
- Can you contact the miner? Emergency hard fork?

## Scenario 4: Founder disappears
- What does the lead have sole access to?
- Who takes over decision-making?
- What about funds/treasury?

## Scenario 5: Legal demand for user data
- What user data do you actually have?
- What's technically possible vs. what you'll comply with?
- Do you have a lawyer on retainer?

## Scenario 6: Hard-fork coordination failure
- 30% of miners haven't upgraded at activation height
- Do you proceed, delay, or cancel?
- Is replay protection baked in?

## Scenario 7: Developer burnout / team dispute
- A core member publicly accuses the project of misconduct
- They fork the project, taking community with them
- How do you respond without escalating?

## Scenario 8: Infrastructure provider terminates service
- DigitalOcean decides crypto violates ToS. 48 hours to move.
- Can you move 10 nodes in 48 hours?
- Do you have multi-provider scripts?

## Scenario 9: Coordinated smear campaign
- "CoinCync has a backdoor" articles appear
- Multiple accounts, similar claims, short timeframe
- How do you respond without amplifying?

---

## How to run

1. Pick one scenario per month
2. Each participant writes what they'd do (10 min)
3. Compare answers — disagreement = gaps (20 min)
4. Identify top 3 gaps
5. Assign someone to close each gap
6. Re-run same scenario in 6 months
