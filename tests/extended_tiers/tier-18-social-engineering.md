# Tier 18 — Social Engineering Response Playbook

---

## Quick reference card

### If something feels urgent, slow down.

### If something asks for:
- Your seed phrase — **reject, always**
- Your private key — **reject, always**
- Your password — **reject, always**
- Urgent action on a link — verify through separate channel first
- Bypassing normal process — verify through separate channel first

### Before sensitive actions, ask:
- Did I verify this request via a different channel?
- Am I doing this because I'm under time pressure?
- Would I do this if I had a day to think about it?

---

## Attack categories

### 1. Impersonation of team members
- Discord/Telegram DMs from fake accounts claiming to be Cync Lab
- Prevention: publish official handles, "we will never DM first asking for funds"

### 2. Fake support
- User posts wallet problem, "helpful" stranger asks for seed phrase
- Prevention: prominent "we never ask for your seed" notices

### 3. Coordinated disinformation
- Multiple accounts: "CoinCync has a backdoor"
- Response: one comprehensive response, don't chase individual claims

### 4. Phishing for team credentials
- Fake security alerts, cloned login pages
- Prevention: YubiKey 2FA on all critical accounts

### 5. Long-term relationship attacks
- "Researcher" builds trust over months, then pushes backdoor
- Prevention: all code requires review, no unilateral commit access

---

## General response

1. **Slow down** — urgency is a red flag
2. **Verify independently** — different channel than the suspicious one
3. **Involve the team** — don't handle alone
4. **Document** — what happened, when, who
5. **Share** — warn community if others might be targeted
