## 2. What CoinCync Does NOT Support

### 2.1 Technical Limitations

CoinCync does NOT provide or support:

- **Fiat currency integration** -- No banking connections, credit card processing, or fiat on/off ramps
- **Custodial services** -- No wallet hosting, key storage, or account recovery services
- **Transaction reversal** -- Confirmed transactions are irreversible; there is no chargeback mechanism
- **Identity recovery** -- Lost private keys cannot be recovered by any party
- **Smart contracts** -- CoinCync is a payment-focused chain, not a programmable blockchain
- **Cross-chain bridges** -- No native support for atomic swaps or wrapped tokens
- **KYC/AML compliance tools** -- The protocol does not collect or verify user identity
- **Merchant services** -- No payment processing, invoicing, or point-of-sale integration
- **Price oracles** -- No native mechanism for external price feeds
- **Governance tokens or voting** -- No on-chain governance mechanism

### 2.2 Privacy Limitations

While CoinCync implements privacy-preserving technology, users should understand:

- **Privacy is not absolute** -- Sophisticated analysis, user error, or metadata leakage may compromise privacy
- **Network-level privacy requires user action** -- Users must configure Tor/I2P themselves; the software does not guarantee IP address protection by default
- **Ring signatures have limitations** -- Small anonymity sets, timing analysis, or repeated spending patterns may reduce effective privacy
- **View keys expose transaction history** -- Sharing view keys with third parties (auditors, exchanges) reveals all incoming transactions
- **Blockchain data is permanent** -- Future cryptographic advances may weaken current privacy guarantees

### 2.3 No Professional Services

CoinCync contributors do NOT provide:

- Legal advice
- Tax advice or reporting
- Investment advice
- Financial planning
- Regulatory compliance consulting
- Security audits or guarantees

## 7. Network Risks

Users acknowledge the following risks:

### 7.1 Technical Risks

- **Consensus failures** -- Bugs in consensus code may cause chain splits or fund loss
- **Cryptographic weaknesses** -- Future attacks may weaken privacy or security
- **51% attacks** -- Sufficient hashpower concentration may enable double-spends
- **Software bugs** -- Undiscovered bugs may cause unexpected behavior
- **Dependency vulnerabilities** -- Third-party libraries may contain security flaws

### 7.2 Economic Risks

- **Value volatility** -- CYNC may lose all value
- **Liquidity risk** -- Markets may not exist to sell CYNC
- **Mining centralization** -- Mining may become concentrated
- **Adoption failure** -- The network may fail to achieve meaningful adoption

### 7.3 External Risks

- **Regulatory bans** -- Governments may prohibit use
- **Exchange delistings** -- Exchanges may refuse to list CYNC
- **ISP blocking** -- Network traffic may be blocked
- **Power grid failures** -- Mining and node operation require electricity
