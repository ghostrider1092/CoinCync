## What's in v1.0.12

Five small consensus tightenings, none of which affect honest
miners producing well-formed blocks. The fork is purely on the
validation side — your blocks stay valid before AND after the fork.

  1. encrypted_amount tightened to exactly 8 bytes
  2. per-output size caps enforced at block-validation level
  3. duplicate stealth addresses rejected within a tx
  4. cross-tx duplicate stealth addresses rejected within a block
  5. ring-size enforcement uses a monotonic counter (closes a
     race-prone live-UTXO-set lookup path)

Full PR: https://github.com/ghostrider1092/Coincync-Testnet-/pull/102
Operator runbook: docs/operations/v1.0.12-hard-fork-rollout.md

## Watching activation

  https://explorer.coincync.network

No miner action required at the activation moment — just have the
v1.0.12-rc1 binary running.

## Why this matters

This is a TESTNET rehearsal. Mainnet launches October 1 with these
rules baked in from genesis — no separate mainnet fork event. The
testnet activation lets us exercise the full announcement →
upgrade → coordinate-activation flow end-to-end with low stakes
before mainnet.

If anything goes sideways, ping me directly in #mining or open an
issue tagged v1.0.12-hard-fork. Thanks for running a node 🛡️
