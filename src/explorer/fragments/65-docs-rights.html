Bill of Rights
The Ten Guaranteed Rights of Every CoinCync User
Version 1.0 · Ratified at Block 0 · Network: CoinCync Mainnet
✓ Irrevocable Block 0 ratified ✓ 10 rights ✓ Permanent
Preamble

These rights belong to every person who uses CoinCync — regardless of who they are, where they live, how much CYNC they hold, or what they use it for.

They were not granted by developers. They cannot be revoked by developers. No governance process, no community vote, no legal order, and no future development team can remove, weaken, suspend, or reinterpret these rights. Any protocol that fails to honor them is not CoinCync.

I
Right 1 of 10
The Right to Financial Privacy
Mathematical guarantee

Every person using CoinCync has the right to conduct financial transactions in private.

The network provides, by default and without exception, the following protections for every transaction: ring signatures to conceal the sender among a set of decoys, stealth addresses to generate one-time destinations that cannot be linked to a public address, Pedersen commitments to conceal transaction amounts, and Bulletproofs to prove amounts are valid without revealing them.

Privacy is not opt-in. There is no transparent mode. Every transaction on CoinCync is private by construction.

II
Right 2 of 10
The Right to Verify Supply
Mathematical guarantee

Every person has the right to independently verify the total supply of CYNC at any block height without trusting any third party.

The Pedersen commitment accumulator built into the CoinCync protocol allows any person to confirm that no coins were created outside the emission schedule, that no transaction created coins from nothing, and that the total supply matches the expected emission at the current block height.

The supply is not something you are asked to trust. It is something you can prove.

III
Right 3 of 10
🖥 The Right to Run a Node
Open source · Community-enforced

No license, permission, fee, identity verification, or approval of any kind shall ever be required to run a CoinCync node.

No person shall be blocked from participating in the CoinCync network based on identity or nationality, geographic location or jurisdiction, political affiliation or beliefs, financial status or holdings, or any other characteristic. The software shall remain open source and freely available.

Participation in the CoinCync network requires nothing but software and an internet connection.

open sourcepermissionlessMIT license
IV
Right 4 of 10
🔑 The Right to Self-Custody
Protocol-enforced · No freeze mechanism

No version of the CoinCync protocol shall ever implement backdoors, key escrow, or any mechanism that allows a third party to access, freeze, seize, or redirect funds.

Your keys are your coins. The protocol has no override. There is no account recovery through a central party. There is no emergency freeze mechanism. There is no kill switch. This right exists because financial self-custody is inseparable from financial privacy — a coin whose funds can be frozen is not a private coin.

No backdoor exists in the protocol. This is verifiable by anyone who reads the source code.

V
Right 5 of 10
🎭 The Right to Pseudonymous Participation
Community-enforced

No part of the CoinCync network, community, or governance shall require identity verification to participate.

Every person may interact with the network, participate in community discussions, submit governance proposals, and contribute to the codebase using a pseudonym. This right exists because the demand for identity verification is the first step toward financial surveillance. Contributors who choose to participate under their real names are equally welcome — the right is to choose, not to be required.

A network that requires you to identify yourself before participating is not a private network.

VI
Right 6 of 10
🚪 The Right to Exit
Protocol-enforced · MIT license

Any person may export their wallet, move their funds, and leave the CoinCync network at any time and for any reason.

No lockup, vesting schedule, exit fee, or protocol-level restriction shall ever be imposed on withdrawing funds. This right also extends to the code — because CoinCync is MIT-licensed, any person may fork the protocol, modify it, and run their own version. The right to exit includes the right to take the technology with you. A user who cannot leave freely is not a user. They are a captive.

MIT license guarantees perpetual freedom to fork, modify, and redistribute.

VII
Right 7 of 10
📖 The Right to Audit the Code
Community-enforced · Open source

The full CoinCync codebase shall remain publicly auditable at all times. No obfuscated, closed-source, or proprietary components shall be introduced.

Every person has the right to read the full source code of the node, wallet, and all supporting tools; compile the software themselves from source; verify that compiled binaries match published source code; audit the cryptographic primitives independently; and publish their findings without restriction. Auditability is not a feature. It is what makes all other rights verifiable.

You do not have to trust that the code does what it claims. You can verify it yourself.

VIII
Right 8 of 10
⚖ The Right to Participate in Governance
Community-enforced

Any CYNC holder may submit governance proposals, participate in protocol discussions, and vote on project-layer decisions regardless of holdings size, identity, or standing.

No minimum holding threshold shall be set so high as to effectively exclude ordinary users from governance participation. No process shall be designed to favor large holders, early contributors, or any specific class of participant over others. Governance participation is open to everyone. The protocol belongs to its users — all of them.

One voice is not worth less because it holds fewer coins.

IX
Right 9 of 10
📊 The Right to Fee Transparency
Protocol-enforced · Deterministic

Transaction fees shall be calculated deterministically and disclosed to users in full before any transaction is broadcast.

No hidden fees, no protocol-level fee extraction to a central party, and no fee mechanisms that advantage any party over another shall ever be implemented. A user has the right to know exactly what they are paying before they pay it. Fee changes may be proposed through the CIP process but may never be implemented in a way that directs any portion of fees to developers, foundations, or any centralized entity.

You will always know the fee before you confirm. No surprises.

deterministic feesno hidden feesno fee extraction

Right 10 of 10
🛡 The Right Against Censorship
Protocol-enforced · No blacklist

The CoinCync protocol shall never implement transaction blacklists, address blocking, output freezing, or any mechanism that allows censorship of valid transactions.

A valid transaction — one that satisfies all cryptographic proofs and consensus rules — shall always be eligible for inclusion in a block. No miner, node, developer, or governance body shall have the ability to prevent a valid transaction from ever being mined. This right exists because financial censorship and financial surveillance are two sides of the same coin. A network that can block transactions can control its users. CoinCync shall never control its users.

No blacklist mechanism exists in the protocol. This is verifiable in the source code.

Closing Seal

These ten rights are final. They are not subject to amendment, revision, or repeal by any person, body, or process — now or at any point in the future. They may only be strengthened by technical improvement. They may never be weakened by any means.

Any version of the CoinCync software that removes or weakens any of these rights is not CoinCync.
