What is CoinCync?
A 2-minute intro for newcomers

CoinCync is a privacy cryptocurrency. Every transaction hides the sender, receiver, and amount by default — no opt-in required.

How is that possible?

  • CLSAG ring signatures — your real identity hides among 10 decoys
  • Stealth addresses — every payment goes to a unique one-time address
  • Pedersen commitments — amounts are hidden but provably balanced
  • Bulletproofs+ — proves amounts are valid without revealing them

Key facts:

  • 100 million CYNC max supply — no inflation
  • CPU mining only (RandomX) — no ASICs, no GPUs
  • 0% dev tax, 0% premine — all coins are mined fairly
  • Mainnet launches October 1, 2026
